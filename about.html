About MyFaceFate

🔮 What is MyFaceFate?

MyFaceFate is a free web app that gives you a playful face reading based on real measurements of your face. It's not a random horoscope: our AI finds 478 points on your face, measures the proportions of your brows, eyes, nose, lips and jaw, and interprets them through the systems of the three great classics of Chinese face reading (Mian Xiang, 面相) — Ma Yi Shen Xiang, Liu Zhuang Xiang Fa and Shen Xiang Quan Bian.

📚 What you'll find

  • Read My Face: your Five Element face type, a feature-by-feature reading and 10 luck scores — money, love, marriage, career, study, health, business, fame, family and social luck.
  • Face Map: the Twelve Palaces of the face and the Five Officials (brows, eyes, nose, mouth, ears).
  • Luck Guide: where each kind of luck "lives" on your face, plus easy glow-up tips.
  • Famous Faces: fun readings of world-famous faces, 36 icons from Abraham Lincoln and Marie Curie to Audrey Hepburn and Bruce Lee.

💛 Our good-vibes promise

Every face is a lucky face. MyFaceFate is designed to be kind: there are no "bad" features, only different strengths. We never comment on race, ethnicity or attractiveness, and every reading is written to make you smile.

🔒 Your photo stays with you

Your photo is analyzed entirely on your own phone or computer and is never uploaded or stored anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for details.

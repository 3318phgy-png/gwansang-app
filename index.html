FAQ

Good questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photo is analyzed entirely inside your own browser and is never sent to or stored on any server. Close the page and it's gone.

Is it random?

Nope. The reading is based on real measurements of your face, so the same photo always gives the same result. Different photos (angle, lighting, expression) can shift things a little — just like your mood does!

What is Chinese face reading?

Face reading (Mian Xiang, 面相) is a centuries-old tradition that links facial features to personality and fortune. MyFaceFate reimagines three classic texts in a modern, fun, always-positive way.

Is this scientific?

No — and we're upfront about it. MyFaceFate is for entertainment only. It's a fun way to look at your face through an ancient lens, not a scientific, medical or psychological assessment.

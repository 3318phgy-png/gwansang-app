Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

MyFaceFate ("we," "us," or "the Service") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Service handles and how.

1. We never upload or store your face photo

Photos you take or upload are analyzed only inside your own browser on your device. Your photo, facial measurements and reading results are never sent to our servers or any third party, and are never stored. When you close or refresh the page, they're gone. We do not create or keep biometric identifiers or face templates of any kind.

2. Information we collect directly

There is no sign-up, and we don't directly collect your name, phone number or email address. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them afterward.

3. Camera access

Your browser asks for camera permission only when you tap "Take a Selfie." You can always use "Upload a Photo" instead. Camera video is never recorded or transmitted.

4. Advertising and cookies

To keep MyFaceFate free, we show ads from third parties such as Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the face-reading features still work normally.

5. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. California residents may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we don't store photos or accounts, most data related to you is held by our advertising partners — you can manage it through the links above, or contact us and we'll help.

6. Other services

We use Google Fonts to display text, which may share technical data (such as your IP address) with Google. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and operations for a limited time.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
